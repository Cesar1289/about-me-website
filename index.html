My name is Cesar Espitia, and I enjoy doing a lot of different things in my free time. I really like working on cars and painting them because I enjoy being creative and learning how things work. I also like playing video games and helping my dad with his work. Whenever I have the chance, I like to play football for fun and spend time being active.

Outside of school I am someone who values personal growth, responsibility, and learning new skills. I enjoy challenging myself with new experiences and finding opportunities to improve both personally and academically. I take pride in being dependable, working well with others, and staying committed to the goals I set for myself. I am always interested in developing new skills and gaining experiences that can help prepare me for my future.

Looking forward, I aim to bridge the gap between what I learn and the real world around me. Whether I am working on cars, helping others, or building new skills, my goal is to keep growing, stay curious, and make the people around me proud.
